## 1. Milford Track
**Location:** Fiordland National Park  
**Distance:** 53.5 km | **Duration:** 4 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate  
**Season:** Late Oct – late Apr  
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Milford+Track+Fiordland+New+Zealand) | [DOC Booking](https://www.doc.govt.nz/milfordtrack)

Often called "the finest walk in the world." The trail winds through ancient beech forests, past towering waterfalls, and over the dramatic Mackinnon Pass. Ends at Milford Sound. Hut booking is mandatory.

---

## 2. Routeburn Track
**Location:** Mt. Aspiring & Fiordland National Parks  
**Distance:** 32 km | **Duration:** 2–4 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate  
**Season:** Late Oct – late Apr  
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Routeburn+Track+New+Zealand) | [DOC Booking](https://www.doc.govt.nz/routeburntrack)

One of New Zealand's Great Walks, connecting two national parks through stunning alpine scenery. Highlights include Lake Harris, Routeburn Falls, and panoramic views from Harris Saddle.

---

## 3. Kepler Track
**Location:** Te Anau, Fiordland National Park  
**Distance:** 60 km | **Duration:** 4 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate–Hard  
**Season:** Late Oct – late Apr (alpine section); year-round (lower sections)  
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kepler+Track+Te+Anau+New+Zealand) | [DOC Booking](https://www.doc.govt.nz/keplertrack)

A circular Great Walk starting and ending at Te Anau. Features alpine ridgelines with sweeping views of Fiordland, deep beech forest, and Lake Te Anau. A great loop option — no shuttle needed.

---

## 4. Rob Roy Track
**Location:** Wanaka, Mt. Aspiring National Park  
**Distance:** 10 km return | **Duration:** 4–5 hours | **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate  
**Season:** Year-round (may be snow in winter)  
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rob+Roy+Track+Wanaka+New+Zealand) | [DOC Info](https://www.doc.govt.nz/robroy)

A spectacular day hike into the Rob Roy Glacier valley. Passes swing bridges, waterfalls, and ends with close-up views of the hanging glacier. One of the best day walks in the South Island — no booking required.

---

## 5. Ben Lomond Track
**Location:** Queenstown  
**Distance:** 11 km return | **Duration:** 5–6 hours | **Difficulty:** Hard  
**Season:** Year-round (snow gear needed in winter)  
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ben+Lomond+Track+Queenstown+New+Zealand) | [DOC Info](https://www.doc.govt.nz/benlomond)

The most rewarding hike accessible directly from Queenstown. A steep climb to 1,748 m with 360° views over Lake Wakatipu, the Remarkables, and Cecil Peak. Take the Skyline Gondola up to save 400 m of climbing. No booking required.
