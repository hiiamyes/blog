# 10 Hostels in South New Zealand

A list of great hostels to stay at while exploring the South Island of New Zealand.

---

## 1. YHA Queenstown Lakefront
**Location:** Queenstown  
**Address:** 88-90 Lake Esplanade, Queenstown  
Right on the shores of Lake Wakatipu, this is one of the most scenic hostels in New Zealand. Offers dorms and private rooms with stunning mountain and lake views.

---

## 2. Haka Lodge Queenstown
**Location:** Queenstown  
**Address:** 8 Fernhill Road, Queenstown  
A modern, social hostel with great facilities including a fully equipped kitchen, lounge, and free Wi-Fi. Popular with adventure travelers heading to Remarkables or Coronet Peak.

---

## 3. Base Backpackers Queenstown
**Location:** Queenstown  
**Address:** Cnr Camp & Shotover Streets, Queenstown  
Central location in the heart of Queenstown. Well known for its lively atmosphere and proximity to bars, restaurants, and adventure booking offices.

---

## 4. YHA Christchurch
**Location:** Christchurch  
**Address:** 36 Hereford Street, Christchurch  
A well-run hostel in central Christchurch, conveniently located near the Botanic Gardens and Hagley Park. Great base for exploring the rebuilt city center.

---

## 5. Rolleston House YHA
**Location:** Christchurch  
**Address:** 5 Worcester Boulevard, Christchurch  
A charming heritage building hostel in the cultural precinct of Christchurch, close to the Arts Centre and Canterbury Museum.

---

## 6. YHA Dunedin
**Location:** Dunedin  
**Address:** 71 Stafford Street, Dunedin  
A comfortable, centrally located hostel in one of New Zealand's most vibrant student cities. Close to the Otago Peninsula wildlife experiences including yellow-eyed penguins and albatross.

---

## 7. Nomads Queenstown
**Location:** Queenstown  
**Address:** 5 Church Street, Queenstown  
A large, well-equipped backpackers hostel with a rooftop bar and social vibe. Central location makes it easy to access all of Queenstown's activities.

---

## 8. YHA Te Anau
**Location:** Te Anau  
**Address:** 29 Mokonui Street, Te Anau  
The perfect base for Milford Sound day trips and Fiordland National Park treks including the famous Milford and Kepler Tracks. Cozy and well-organized.

---

## 9. YHA Wanaka
**Location:** Wanaka  
**Address:** 94 Brownston Street, Wanaka  
A relaxed hostel in the charming lakeside town of Wanaka. Great for hiking, skiing, and exploring Mt. Aspiring National Park. More laid-back alternative to Queenstown.

---

## 10. Kaikoura Top 10 Holiday Park & Motels
**Location:** Kaikoura  
**Address:** 34 Beach Road, Kaikoura  
A hostel and holiday park right by the sea in Kaikoura, famous for whale watching and swimming with dolphins. Excellent starting point for marine wildlife encounters on the east coast of the South Island.
