## 1. YHA Queenstown Lakefront
**Location:** Queenstown  
**Address:** 88-90 Lake Esplanade, Queenstown  
**Price:** Dorm ~$38–$48 / Private ~$110–$140 NZD per night  
**Links:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=YHA+Queenstown+Lakefront+88+Lake+Esplanade+Queenstown) | [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=YHA+Queenstown+Lakefront)

Right on the shores of Lake Wakatipu, this is one of the most scenic hostels in New Zealand. Offers dorms and private rooms with stunning mountain and lake views.

**Location:** Queenstown  
**Address:** 8 Fernhill Road, Queenstown  
**Price:** Dorm ~$32–$45 / Private ~$100–$130 NZD per night
